How to Use This Resource

A quick-reference companion to the full I AM Caring and Curious Companion, not a replacement for it.

What this is

Each grade level in this packet has its own single page: the one Information, one Media, and one AI focus for that year, the one Care, one Curiosity, and one Connection capacity, and the one Identity & Presence, one Privacy & Agency, and one Wellbeing & Intentionality responsibility. Nine anchors, at a glance, for a single grade. It’s designed to be printed, hung in a classroom, or kept in a planning folder: something to glance at, not read cover to cover.

How each page is organized

Every grade page has the same three rows, top to bottom, and the order is deliberate, not alphabetical:

LAYER 1 Converging Literacies What a student needs to know and do.
←
LAYER 2 · THE DRIVER Human-Centered Capacities Why a student would want to use those skills at all.
→
LAYER 3 Connected World Responsibilities How a student carries that knowledge responsibly.

Layer 2 sits in the middle of every page because it drives the other two. Care, Curiosity, and Connection aren’t a rest stop between what a student knows (Layer 1) and how they act on it (Layer 3); they’re the engine. A student verifies a source, questions an AI output, or reflects on their online presence because they care about getting it right, stay curious enough to keep asking, and feel connected enough to the people the answer affects. Take Layer 2 out and Layer 1 becomes compliance and Layer 3 becomes a rule to follow, rather than a reason worth following it.

That’s why, on each page, the middle row isn’t just visually centered: it’s the row worth starting from if you only have time to look at one.

Within each cell, the bold line is the grade-level essential itself. The italic line below it is a plain-language gloss, for anyone who wants a quick reminder of what the term means.

How to use it

  • Pick one to three of the nine items for any single lesson. That’s the limit for one shift, not for the year.
  • Across the whole year, the hope is that all nine get used somewhere, spread across different lessons rather than crammed into one.
  • Fold your pick into a lesson you’re already teaching. This is meant to deepen existing instruction, not add a new one.
  • Use the Teacher Think Sheet (next page) to plan the shift: what stays the same, the one small change, and what you’ll notice if it’s working.
  • When students create, invite them to use the Creator Think Sheet in the online companion before they share or publish their work.

A note on Layer 3. Identity & Presence, Privacy & Agency, and Wellbeing & Intentionality now have their own statement at every grade, grouped under four band mottos (for example, “Grades 3–5 · I build and protect.”). Each responsibility moves from me toward us: from how I show up and protect myself toward how I treat, protect, and support others. Ethics lives here too, most visibly in Identity & Presence.

A note on Care and Connection. Care includes how a student feels, not only what they notice in the world around them. And Connection’s call to understand people who see things differently is not a condition for being heard: a student who is hurting is not first asked to understand the other side.

Nine anchors for the year. You don't need all nine at once: pick one to three and build them into a lesson you already teach.

LAYER 1 Converging
Literacies
INFORMATION

Ask: "How do we know this is real?"

The first habit of information literacy: pause and ask where something came from before believing it.

MEDIA

Identify a logo or brand

Logos are visual clues about who made or is selling something.

AI

Know that computers can make pictures, voices, and words that look and sound real

Computers can make convincing pictures and voices. Even adults can't always tell by looking, so we ask how we know.

Grade 11

Nine anchors for the year. You don't need all nine at once: pick one to three and build them into a lesson you already teach.

LAYER 1 Converging
Literacies
INFORMATION

Evaluate quality of evidence

Not all evidence is equal. A personal story, a single study, and a research review carry different weight.

MEDIA

Analyze framing

Two accurate stories can feel very different depending on what's emphasized, included, or left out.

AI

Evaluate who benefits and who is harmed, including environmental costs

AI has real winners, losers, and environmental costs. Asking who pays is a civic question.

LAYER 2 Human-
Centered
Capacities
CARE

I can weigh competing things I care about and explain which one I'm choosing to prioritize, and why.

The capacity to sincerely care about people, things, and how a student feels: the spark everything else starts from.

CURIOSITY

I follow a question across more than one field or discipline to understand it fully.

What caring turns into when it meets the world's questions: the active habit of asking and pursuing, not a fixed trait.

CONNECTION

I can identify what a perspective I disagree with gets right, not only what it gets wrong.

Discovering how what a student cares about and is curious about is relevant to themselves and others, without their own feelings needing to wait for that understanding first.

World

I AM Caring and Curious · Teacher One-Page

Grade 12

Nine anchors for the year. You don't need all nine at once: pick one to three and build them into a lesson you already teach.

LAYER 1 Converging
Literacies
INFORMATION

Synthesize conflicting claims

Synthesis means understanding why sources disagree and forming an honest position, not just picking a side.

MEDIA

Create and critically evaluate counter-narratives

A counter-narrative should be more honest than what it challenges, not just more emotionally persuasive.

AI

Apply human oversight, including civic and democratic accountability

"The AI decided" is never a final answer. Humans stay responsible for real-world consequences.

Centered

The capacity to sincerely care about people, things, and how a student feels: the spark everything else starts from.

CURIOSITY

I pursue a question because it matters to me, not because it was assigned.

What caring turns into when it meets the world's questions: the active habit of asking and pursuing, not a fixed trait.

CONNECTION

Making informed decisions about information, their own and other people's, and how it's used.
